[h1]Cosmic web[/h1]
about 35000 real galaxies from the 2MASS Redshift Survey across the whole sky, out to about 300 Mpc
together with space engines own galaxies and the clusters above they trace the real cosmic web: the walls, filaments, superclusters and voids around us
galaxies in groups are placed at their groups distance so clusters dont stretch into long streaks
sources:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.0669
https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.03134

deep cosmic web (optional, big file)
96117 more real galaxies from the 6dF Galaxy Survey in the southern sky, out to about 850 Mpc
if space engine gets slow, delete only the file "178 Cosmic Web Deep (6dF).sc"
sources:
https://arxiv.org/abs/0903.5451

[h1]Zone of Avoidance[/h1]
3788 real galaxies hidden behind the disk of the Milky Way, the band across the sky that the big surveys skip
from the 2MZoA catalogue, the HIZOA radio survey and the MeerKAT Vela and galactic plane surveys, built to fill the gap the 2MASS Redshift Survey leaves along the galactic plane
every galaxy has its own measured redshift, so it sits at its true distance (galaxies with no redshift are left out, because their distance is unknown)
sources:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26979
https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202556243
